# Dock Talk: Autumn Newsletter

## Welcome

Welcome to the autumn edition. It has been a busy few months and this issue covers the new Telford dock layout, some changes to peak season working, and a few reminders from the safety team.

## Telford dock reopens

After eight weeks of works, Dock 3 at Telford reopened on 26 October. The new layout has separated pedestrian walkways, a dedicated trailer holding bay and a second tail-lift ramp. Early feedback from drivers has been very positive, and the average turnaround time is already down by about twenty minutes. Thank you to everyone who put up with the noise and the diversions.

Please remember that pedestrians must use the green walkways at all times. The old shortcut past the cardboard baler is now closed.

## Peak season

Peak starts on 23 November. Shifts will move to a six-day pattern for most teams, and holiday requests for the period 23 November to 24 December will not normally be approved. If you have a pressing reason, speak to your supervisor before 16 November. Agency staff will be inducted on 18 and 19 November, and we need volunteers to act as buddies. If you can help, please email the shift office.

## Safety corner

- Check your handheld battery at the start of every shift.
- Report any damaged pallets at once; do not stack them.
- High-visibility jackets must be worn on the shop floor, including by visitors.
- Forklift pre-use checks must be signed off before the truck leaves the charging bay.
