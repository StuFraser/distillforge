1. NEW STOCK RECEIPT PROCESS

Our current goods-in process was designed when we had around forty suppliers. We're now at about a hundred and thirty, and from January we're bringing in two new contract manufacturers in Poland and Vietnam. Goods-in is the bottleneck. We regularly have trailers waiting over two hours to tip, and our receiving accuracy has dropped to around ninety-four percent, mostly quantity errors and items booked to the wrong PO line.

What we'd like the new process to look like is roughly as follows. Please treat this as a starting point rather than a spec, as Helen and her team will want to work through the detail with you.

Advance shipping notices. We want our larger suppliers to send ASNs, either via EDI or uploaded through a supplier portal, so that the inbound is already expected at line level before the truck arrives. Receiving should then be a case of scanning the SSCC on each pallet and confirming, rather than counting everything from scratch. For suppliers who can't send ASNs we'll keep receiving against the PO, but the handheld flow needs to be the same as far as possible.

Dock scheduling. Suppliers or hauliers should be able to book a dock slot, and the goods-in team should see a daily schedule of what's arriving and when. Owen has been asking for this for a long time.

Over and under receipts. At the moment the system lets anyone receive any quantity. We want configurable tolerances per supplier, with anything outside tolerance held for a supervisor to approve. Partial receipts must leave the PO line open, and we need a way to close a line short when the supplier confirms the balance won't be sent.

Quality inspection. Certain parts, mostly the precision machined items and anything for our aerospace customers, need to be inspected before they can be put away. These should go to a QC hold location automatically based on the item or supplier, and stay unavailable for allocation until QC releases them. If they fail inspection we need to raise a supplier return from the same screen.

Cross-docking. Some inbound lines are already allocated to urgent customer orders or to a works order on the shop floor. Where that's the case, the receiver should be told at the point of receipt to send it straight to despatch or to the production line, rather than putting it away and picking it again an hour later.

Labelling and putaway. We'd like the system to print an internal licence plate label at receipt and suggest a putaway location based on the item's storage rules, rather than relying on operators' knowledge.

Business Central. Receipts need to post back to the PO in Business Central in near real time, including partial receipts, so that accounts payable can do three-way matching. Today there's a delay of up to a day, which causes problems at month end.

Timing-wise, we'd ideally like the new process in place before the Poland and Vietnam volumes start ramping up, which is the beginning of March. Could you let me know whether that's realistic, what you'd need from us, and whether any of this needs a change request or additional licences? A rough plan by the end of the month would be really helpful, as I need to include it in our Q1 budget submission on the 4th of December.
